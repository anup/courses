Programming contest

The weekly Wednesday CodeChef contest, 8:00–10:00 PM. It is held in the labs (Rooms 10/104, 10/105, 10/203, 10/204) and is proctored — you must be present in the lab to take it. It cannot be done remotely. The best (N − 3) contests count.

How each weekly contest is scored

Each weekly CodeChef contest is scored out of 10 marks, based on your attendance and how many problems you solve during the contest — not on your CodeChef rating, division, or rank.

Important: log in to CodeChef with the exact username you submitted in the handle form. Submissions from any other account or username will not count toward your marks.

  • Attend the contest (present in the lab): +2 (running total 2)
  • Solve your 1st problem: +3 (running total 5)
  • Solve your 2nd problem: +2 (running total 7)
  • Solve your 3rd problem: +2 (running total 9)
  • Solve your 4th problem: +1 (running total 10)

“1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th” is the count of problems you solve, not specific problem IDs — solving any four problems earns the full 10. Problems beyond the fourth add no marks, but you should still solve them: you will not be allowed to leave the room until you have solved all the problems in your division (or until the contest ends at 10:00 PM).

Your rating and division do not affect your exam marks — only how many problems you solve. If you are not in Division 4, you can still solve Division 4’s problems as they show up in the “Non-Scorable” section below the problems list on the contest page of your division. Solving the Non-Scorable problems will not change your CodeChef rank, but each one still counts toward your exam marks.

Your final Programming contest score is the average of your best (N − 3) contests (your three weakest are dropped).
