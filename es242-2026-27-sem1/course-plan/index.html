Course Plan

ES 242: Data Structures and Algorithms I.

Schedule Classes run from 4 August to 19 November 2026. Lectures: Tuesday and Friday, 11:30-12:50, 10/103. Lab: Monday, 14:00-15:20, 10/104, 10/105, 10/203, and 10/204.
Staff Manoj D Gupta leads the course. Anup Kalbalia handles labs.
Topics Big O; C arrays; insertion sort; merge sort; quick sort; heap sort; C pointers and structures; linked lists; stacks and queues; augmenting data structures and hash tables; graphs.
Tentative Grading Labs: 50%, counting best N - 3 labs. Mid-semester exam: 20%. End-semester exam: 20%. CodeChef Starters: 10%, counting best 10 contests. This policy is subject to minor updates.
Programming Contests Wednesday CodeChef Starters will be used for the contest component. The per-contest scoring scale will follow the previous ES 242 setup.
