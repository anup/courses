Class 09 · Tuesday 8 September

The Hackattic Contest

Scored, as a group. If even one person in your group cannot explain a challenge, it does not count.

Setup

  • Every member needs their own hackattic account. No account, no points.
  • pip install websockets — the only install.

The challenges

ChallengePoints
Collision course15
WebSocket chit chat30
Visual basic math35
The one with Redis70

Scoring

Your score is the total points of the challenges you clear on your own account.

Your team's score is the average of its members' scores.

Rules

1You compete as your group.
2Everyone solves every challenge on their own account.
3Any AI, any model, any tool. The viva is the check.
4Talk within your group, not across groups.
5Rejected submissions cost nothing. Retry freely.
6Any order. You may abandon a challenge and come back.
7If hackattic errors or rate-limits you, tell an instructor at the time.

Commit by end of day

Group repo — through a pull request, approved by a different member. Nothing goes straight to main.

  • hackattic/<challenge>/solve.py — one agreed solution per challenge your group cleared. You each wrote your own; compare them and commit the one you agree is best.
  • hackattic/NOTES.md — each of you adds your own short summary: what tripped you up on each challenge and how you got past it. All of you editing one file will conflict. Resolve it.
  • No access token in any file.

Your own repo

  • hackattic/<challenge>/solve.py — your own solution, the one that cleared it on your account. Not your group's agreed version.
  • lab-notes/class-09.md, written in “I”, not “we”.
