Class 14 · Monday 5 October

Find Your User

The Open Hackathon starts today. Your group builds one piece of software until 17 November, for a real person who will use it. Today you find that person and decide what to build for them. The code comes later.

1. Read the hackathon page (5 minutes)

All of it: the Open Hackathon. The rules and dates there hold for the rest of the semester.

2. Pick what to build (25 minutes)

Start from a person, not a technology: someone you know whose week has a job that is slow, messy or still done on paper. Talk it through as a group and settle on one idea that passes the three tests on the hackathon page.

3. Write it in three sentences (10 minutes)

  1. Who the user is: a named person or role, not "students".
  2. What they do today, without your software.
  3. What your software changes for them.

Show it to an instructor before you leave.

4. Your first five statements (30 minutes)

A spec statement says one thing the user can do or one thing that must never happen, in a way a test can check. Draft five now. They go into SPEC.md once your repo exists.

Cannot be checkedCan be checked
The app is easy to use.A student can book a court in at most three clicks from the home page.
Bookings are handled properly.Two students cannot book the same court for the same hour.
It is fast.The list of today's bookings loads in under one second with 500 bookings stored.
Admins can manage things.The sports secretary can cancel any booking; a student can cancel only their own.

For each statement, ask: what would a test do, and what would it look for? If you cannot say, rewrite the statement.

5. Tonight, and this week

  1. Tonight, by 23:59, one person posts in the CS437 space: your group name and the three sentences.
  2. This week, talk to your user. Ask what they do today, what annoys them about it, and what they would want first. Write one thing they said, in their words, at the top of your SPEC.md (due Friday 9 October).

If two groups post the same idea, the group that posted first builds it, and the other picks something else. Ideas are approved on Tuesday 6 October.

6. Before you leave

In your own repo, lab-notes/class-14.md, in "I": the two people you named, which idea you argued for and why, and the statement you wrote.

Class 09 vivas continue today for G1, G2, G7, G8 and G9. If your group is called, go; the rest of your group carries on.
