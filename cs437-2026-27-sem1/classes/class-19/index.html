2. Hackathon: what will be marked

Most of the hackathon marks go to usability: an app that is deployed, and that people outside your group actually use. A deployed app with real users counts for much more than code or tests that nobody else runs.

At every check-in, from today, we will ask two things:

  • Where is it deployed? Show us the link.
  • Who outside your group has used it, and how many people?

3. Group check-ins

We meet each group in turn after the guest session. Bring your deployed link (or what stops you deploying), your repo open, and what each member has merged since 26 October.
